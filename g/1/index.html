How to use Gemini's new Tasks mode

Hand your errands to Google's AI in about 5 minutes. Step by step, no tech skills needed.

Google just added Tasks to the Gemini app. Instead of chatting back and forth, you describe a goal, tap a button, and Gemini keeps working on it in the background, even when you're away from your computer. Here's exactly how to set it up.

✅ Before you start

  • You're 18 or older.
  • You're signed in with a personal Google account (work and school accounts don't work yet).
  • You have a paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plan (per Google's Oct 13, 2026 announcement).
  • You're not in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria. Tasks isn't available there yet.
  • You're using the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, or gemini.google.com.

📱 Step by step

  1. Open Gemini on your phone, or go to gemini.google.com on your computer.
  2. Tap "Task". On your phone it's just above the text box. On a computer it's just below it.
  3. Describe your goal in plain words. Say what you want and when. For example: "every Sunday evening" or "after my flight lands".
  4. Submit and let it run. You can close the app.
  5. Check in. Open the menu and tap the task's thread to see progress or add instructions. If Gemini needs you (like confirming a booking), it will ask you to take over.

🔌 Connect your apps

Type @ followed by the app name (like @Instacart) or just ask Gemini to use it by name. If the app isn't connected yet, Gemini offers to connect it. Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Keep, Photos, Maps, Flights and Hotels work too.

Heads up: Instacart is US-only and English-only for now. Availability of each app depends on your country and device.

✂️ 5 prompts you can copy and paste

  1. Weekly planner: "Every Sunday at 6pm, look at my Google Calendar for the coming week and email me a short summary with anything I need to prepare."
  2. Groceries: "@Instacart Add everything I need for a simple lasagna for 4 people to my cart. Don't check out. Show me the cart first."
  3. Dinner plans: "@OpenTable Find a table for 2 near me this Friday around 7pm. Give me 3 options before booking anything."
  4. Flight watcher: "Check Google Flights every morning for round trips from New York to Los Angeles, Dec 20 to 27, and tell me if a fare drops under $250."
  5. Inbox catch-up: "Go through my Gmail from this week, list every email that still needs a reply, and draft a short reply for each. Don't send anything."

🛟 Stay in control

  • Always add "don't check out" or "don't send" until you trust it. Review before anything costs money.
  • To pause everything: Settings → Task settings → Turn off under "Use task mode to hand off work". Your tasks pause but aren't deleted.

Sources

Google Blog, "Gemini 4 Argon comes to the Gemini app" (Oct 13, 2026) and Gemini Apps Help, "Manage your tasks & workflows in Gemini Apps". Features and availability can change, so check Google's help pages if something looks different.
