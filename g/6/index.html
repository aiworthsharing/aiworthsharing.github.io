Amazon Bee: the AI you wear that takes notes (and the catches)

Amazon's Bee Frontier and Bee Eclipse in plain English: what you get free, what needs Bee Pro, and the privacy controls.

On Oct 15, 2026 Amazon introduced two new AI devices from Bee, which Amazon owns: the Bee Frontier and the Bee Eclipse. Bee is what Amazon calls a "personal AI": it takes note of details from your conversations and turns them into summaries, to-dos, reminders and insights. Here is what Amazon says it does, what is free, and what to know before you buy.

🐝 The two devices

  • Bee Frontier: wear it on your wrist or clip it onto clothes. Colors: Lavender, Sage, Onyx. IP67 water resistance, up to 7 days of battery, USB-C charging. Dual microphones with noise filtering, so it can work in a crowded restaurant or open office. Available now for $74.99 at Amazon.com.
  • Bee Eclipse: about the size of a credit card, attaches magnetically to the back of your phone, more than 21 days of battery. You turn it on during phone calls (Amazon's example: planning a vacation with family). Colors: Silver, Midnight, Honey, Blush. "Available soon", from $99.99 (no date given).

✨ Three things to know

  1. It takes notes for you. It turns conversations into summaries, to-dos and reminders. Amazon's examples: remembering coffee plans made in passing, or buying the dish soap you said you'd buy.
  2. The battery lasts. Up to 7 days on the Frontier, more than 21 days on the Eclipse.
  3. It plugs into your apps. It connects to Gmail, Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook: after a call or meeting it can surface action items, draft follow-up emails and create calendar invites. New: connect to Alexa+ (ask Alexa for a meeting summary, to-dos, or "the name of the book your friend suggested") and to Amazon.com (items you mention show up as product recommendations on the Amazon homepage and in the Bee apps).

💳 Free vs Bee Pro

  • Free with purchase: conversation summaries, recaps and 14-day memory.
  • Bee Pro, $11.99/month, is needed for: unlimited recent summaries, the Amazon.com and Alexa+ integrations, third-party app integrations, suggested to-dos, daily memories, the chat assistant, templates and the desktop app.
  • New customers get 30 days of Bee Pro free.

🔒 Privacy controls

  • A microphone button turns processing on and off, and a visible indicator light shows when it is on.
  • It can turn itself off after no sound (Amazon's examples: 5 or 15 minutes).
  • On calls with the Eclipse, you are prompted to get permission from the people on the call each time a recording starts.
  • You can delete transcripts and summaries at any time.

Amazon's own claims: audio is processed in real time and deleted, never stored; transcripts are encrypted with keys your device controls; and no one, including Amazon, can access your data unless you share it. These are Amazon's statements and have not been independently verified.

⚠️ The catch

  • Most of the useful features (Alexa+, Amazon.com, Gmail and Calendar integrations, suggested to-dos) need Bee Pro at $11.99/month. The free plan keeps only 14 days of memory.
  • The Bee Eclipse is not on sale yet: Amazon says "available soon".
  • It listens to your conversations, and the privacy protections above are Amazon's own claims.
  • If you connect Amazon.com, things you mention can turn into shopping recommendations (this is an optional connection).

Setup

Amazon's announcement does not list step-by-step setup instructions, so none are given here. See Amazon's page below for the latest details.
