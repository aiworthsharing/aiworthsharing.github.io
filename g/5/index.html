Apple HomeView: what the new Siri screen does (and the catches)

Apple's $299 smart home screen with the new Siri AI, explained in plain English: what it does, where it works, and what to know first.

On Oct 13, 2026 Apple announced Apple HomeView: a 7-inch screen with a speaker base for your tabletop (there is also a separate wall-mounted model). It is built around Siri AI, which Apple calls an entirely new version of Siri. Here is what Apple says it does, and what to know before you buy.

🏠 What it is

  • A smart home screen, $299 (U.S.) for both models.
  • Pre-orders started Oct 13 in Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, the UK and the U.S. (Singapore Oct 15). It is available Friday, November 13.
  • Setup needs a compatible iPhone or iPad.

🗣️ What Siri AI does on it

  • Is more conversational and has broad world knowledge, so anyone in the home can ask about virtually any topic. Apple says even people without an Apple Intelligence iPhone get world knowledge, natural conversation and music discovery.
  • With an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone or iPad, it can use personal context (Calendar, Messages, Apple Music playlists) and take action across apps.
  • Conversations you start on HomeView continue in the Siri app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Vision Pro.

✨ Three things to know

  1. It knows your face. It recognizes up to six people, and each gets their own personalized Home Screen. Personal content such as Messages, Calendar, Reminders and Find My only shows when that person's face is recognized and their paired iPhone or iPad is nearby and recently unlocked.
  2. It runs your home. Control by touch or voice: camera feeds, thermostat, lights, door locks and blinds. The Home Camera face can show up to nine camera feeds at once.
  3. It listens for danger. It detects the sound of smoke or carbon monoxide alarms and alerts your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

Also: in the kitchen it turns recipes from the web into step-by-step instructions with hands-free timers. With a supported iCloud+ subscription and compatible cameras, it can recognize people, familiar faces, pets, packages and vehicles, and describe video clips.

⚠️ The catch

  • Not sold in the European Union, because Siri AI is currently unavailable in iOS in the EU.
  • Siri AI on HomeView is in beta (software 27.2).
  • Some Apple Intelligence features that use server-side models have daily usage limits. Apple says expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.
  • Siri AI on HomeView is English only (U.S., Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, UK).
  • Camera recognition needs an iCloud+ subscription, and smart home accessories are sold separately.

🔒 Privacy

Apple says Siri requests use on-device processing or Private Cloud Compute, and that data in Private Cloud Compute "is not stored nor made accessible to Apple or anyone else." That is Apple's own statement; it has not been independently verified.
