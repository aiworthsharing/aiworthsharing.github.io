Anthropic's Public Benefit Fund: what's known and what isn't

5% of Anthropic's shares for the public good: what the fund could do, and what still needs approval.

On Oct 18, 2026 Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, announced the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. This guide covers what Anthropic says it is, what is known, and what is not decided yet. Everything here comes from Anthropic's own announcement.

💡 What it is

Anthropic is committing a portion of its shares to a new entity, the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic's shares will be committed. The board can add another 2% for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline.

✅ What is known

  • The Fund is meant to be flexible. Anthropic says it "might explore" large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations.
  • Workers are one example. Anthropic says AI could generate enormous economic value but could also cause instability for many workers. It has already committed $200 million to studying and addressing this, and says bolder approaches may be needed.
  • It is a company-owned LLC, not a 501(c)(3), to allow different forms of giving. Anthropic says none of the equity held by the LLC will be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad.
  • It builds on earlier pledges. Co-founders pledged to donate 80% of their equity, and about half of employees take part in an equity donation match program. With this announcement, Anthropic says roughly 20% of its shares are dedicated to the public good, plus any extra from valuation milestones.

❓ Not decided yet

  • Whether stockholders approve the 5%.
  • Who would receive money, how much, and when. Anthropic only says what it "might explore".
  • The baseline value and the valuation milestones that could add to the stake.
  • The Fund's structure and governance. Anthropic says it will share more "in the coming months", plus an upcoming public benefit report.

⚠️ The catch

  • It is not final. The 5% depends on stockholder approval.
  • The uses are ideas. Anthropic says it "might explore" them.
  • The extra 2% is the board's choice, and the baseline it depends on has not been shared.
  • Details are still to come. This is Anthropic's own description of its plan.

What you can do

Anthropic's announcement does not ask the public to do anything or to apply for money, so none is given here. Watch Anthropic's news page for the promised update on the Fund's structure and governance.
