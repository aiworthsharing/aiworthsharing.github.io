Turn on Android's AI scam protection in 5 minutes

A simple checklist to switch on every scam shield on your Android phone. Send it to your parents too.

Google says its AI already flags more than 5 million scam calls and texts every month, and new protections are rolling out now. Most are free and built in. You just need to make sure they're on. Here's the checklist.

1. Spam protection in Google Messages

  1. Open Google Messages.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right).
  3. Tap Messages settings → Protection & Safety → Spam protection.
  4. Turn on Enable spam protection.

2. Scam Detection for chat messages

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Search for "Scam Detection". (On a Pixel: Security & privacy → More security & privacy → Scam Detection.)
  3. Make sure Message scams is on. It's on by default, but check.

When a message looks risky you'll see a red warning and a "Likely scam" button.

3. Scam Detection for phone calls

This one is off until you turn it on.

  1. Open the Phone app (Phone by Google).
  2. Tap More → Settings → Scam Detection.
  3. Turn on Scam Detection.

4. The new "Enhanced protection" mode

Google announced a new opt-in Enhanced protection setting in Google Messages on Oct 14, 2026. It uses Gemini to read suspicious conversations with strangers and check brand-new links before you tap. It's rolling out over the coming weeks, and the smarter link checks need newer phones (like Pixel 10 and Galaxy S26). When it reaches your phone, look for it in your Google Messages settings. We'll update this guide when it's live.

5. Check a suspicious message yourself

  • Circle to Search: touch and hold the Home button or the bar at the bottom of the screen, circle the message, and read the analysis.
  • Google Lens: screenshot the message, open the Google app, tap Lens, pick the screenshot, and tap Search.

6. Things that protect you automatically

  • Screen-share privacy: Android hides passwords and payment fields in Chrome while you share your screen. Rolling out this month on Android 15 and newer.
  • Call-forwarding protection: blocks apps from secretly forwarding your calls and warns you about forwarding codes. Coming in early December on Pixel and select phones.
  • Verified bank calls: with partner banks, Android checks whether the bank really called. If not, it hangs up and warns you.

Want the maximum?

Turn on Advanced Protection: Settings → Security & Privacy → Other settings → Advanced Protection → turn on Device protection. It locks in these defenses with one switch. You'll need a screen lock, and your phone may restart.

No tool catches every scam. Never share codes, passwords, or card numbers with someone who contacted you first.

Sources

Google Blog, "New Android protections to help keep you safe from mobile scams" (Oct 14, 2026), and Google Help Center pages for Google Messages spam protection, Scam Detection, the Phone app, and Advanced Protection. Menu names can vary by phone maker.
